How this is calculated
  • Both paths start with the same cash. The buyer spends it on the down payment and closing costs; the renter invests it.
  • Each month, whichever path costs less invests the difference at your investment return.
  • At the end the buyer sells: home value minus selling costs minus the remaining loan, plus any investments. The renter keeps their investments. No capital gains tax is applied to the home.
  • The most you could buy for your rent holds your down payment percentage, tax rate and insurance rate constant.
